        Welcome to Markets

        See your TSX and US stocks together, in Canadian dollars: today's change, total return, allocation and a chart of your whole portfolio.

        Live prices need a free key from Finnhub. It takes about a minute, and the next steps walk you through it.

        Get your free Finnhub key

        Finnhub gives live US stock prices for free: up to 60 updates a minute, with no daily cap.

        1. Tap Open Finnhub. It opens in a new tab.
        2. Sign up with your email (free, no card) and confirm it.
        3. Your API key is on your Finnhub dashboard. Copy it.
        Open Finnhub

        Paste your Finnhub key

        It's saved only in this browser and only ever sent to Finnhub. You can change it later in Settings.

        Toronto stocks and charts (optional)

        Finnhub's free plan covers US stocks only. For TSX prices and price charts, add a free Alpha Vantage key too. Those prices are delayed and limited to 25 requests a day, so Markets saves them up.

        About Markets

        I wanted one place to see my TSX and US stocks together, in Canadian dollars, without signing up for anything. So I built it.

        How it works

        You add a symbol, how many shares you own and what you paid. Markets gets live US prices from Finnhub about once a minute, Toronto prices and daily history from Alpha Vantage, and the USD to CAD rate from Frankfurter. Then it works out your value, today's change, total return and allocation, and the portfolio chart adds up each holding's daily closes. Everything is saved in your own browser; nothing goes to a server of mine.

        What it won't do

        It won't trade, connect to a brokerage, or tell you what to buy. It doesn't count fees, dividends, cash or taxes, so total return here isn't your full investment performance.

        Built with AI coding tools; I decide what it does and review and test each change. See the code or go back to marcantoniopovolo.com.

What it is

Markets shows my TSX and US stocks together, in Canadian dollars: live US prices, today’s change, total return, allocation and a chart of the whole portfolio. It installs on a phone or computer like a regular app, walks new users through getting a free data key, and keeps keys and holdings in the browser. Visitors without a key see a demo portfolio.

It started as a local Python and SQLite app, became a web app called Investing Tracker, and is now Markets by MP.

How it works

  • Live US prices come from Finnhub’s free plan, about once a minute.
  • Toronto prices and daily history come from Alpha Vantage, which allows 25 requests a day, so the app saves them and only asks when it needs to.
  • The USD to CAD rate comes from Frankfurter, which needs no key.
  • The maths (value, return, allocation and the portfolio chart) lives in one module with its own tests.
  • Everything is saved in your own browser. Nothing goes to a server of mine.
